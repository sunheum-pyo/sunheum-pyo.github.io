Privacy Policy

ytm-playlist is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses the owner's YouTube account only to search for videos, create playlists, and add videos to them.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computers.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
